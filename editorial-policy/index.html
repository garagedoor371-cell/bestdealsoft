Our process

Editorial policy: how we review AppSumo deals

Every review on BestDealSoft follows the same process. This page covers what we check, what you’ll get in a review, what we won’t do, and how to flag an error.

The short version

We read the live listing and the deal terms, write down the limits, and say who the deal is for. Every review shows the date it was last checked. We don’t publish until the review is done, and we don’t dress up vendor copy as our own opinion.

How we evaluate a deal

Check the live listing

Product page, tier structure, price, seat and code limits, refund window. We record the date we checked.

Read the vendor docs

What the product does today, not what the roadmap promises. Unshipped features don’t count toward a verdict.

Map the tier limits

Codes, stacks, seats, feature restrictions per tier, and the downgrade rules. Tier caps are where lifetime deals most often disappoint.

Match against real use cases

Who should buy, who should skip, and which tier makes sense. A deal can be a good product and still be the wrong buy for you.

Publish with a check date

Each review shows its last-checked date and links the live listing, so you can confirm the terms before you pay.

Recheck and correct

Deal terms change. When they do, or when a reader reports an error, we update the review and note the correction date.

What every review will include

  • A one-line verdict you can act on.
  • Who the deal suits, and who should skip it.
  • Deal terms and tier caps as checked, with the date.
  • The trade-offs and the features that are missing or gated.
  • A link to the live AppSumo listing so you can verify the current price and terms.
  • The date the review was last updated.

What we won’t do

  • Claim hands-on testing that didn’t happen. Where a review relies on vendor material rather than our own use, it says so.
  • Guess prices, tier caps, or refund terms. If we can’t verify a term, we say it’s unverified and link the source.
  • Sell placement or bump a deal for payment. Commission never decides a verdict. See our affiliate disclosure.
  • Copy vendor marketing as our own words. Features are restated in plain language, and limits are never omitted to make a listing look better.

Corrections

Spotted an error? Email hello@bestdealsoft.com with the page link and what’s wrong. Sources help. We fix confirmed errors and note the correction date on the page. You can also use the contact page for anything else.

Common questions

Is BestDealSoft affiliated with AppSumo?

No. BestDealSoft is an independent publication. It has no connection to AppSumo LLC, and AppSumo is a third-party marketplace.

Do affiliate links change your verdicts?

No. If a link on this site earns us a commission, the review says the same thing it would otherwise. Affiliate links are marked, and our affiliate disclosure explains the details.

Why are there no reviews yet?

We’re in launch. We publish a review only when it’s finished and checked, so the first ones take time. The review library stays empty until then, on purpose.

How often are reviews updated?

When deal terms change, when a product ships major changes, or when a reader reports an error. Every review shows the date it was last checked, and corrections get a dated note.
